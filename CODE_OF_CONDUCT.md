# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place for designers and developers of every
background and experience level.

**Do:** be kind and patient, give feedback on the work and not the person,
credit other people's work, and respect the cultures and traditions that
designs draw on.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; post sexual, violent or hateful
content; share other people's private information; or pass off someone
else's work as your own.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
connected to this project.

**Reporting:** email contact@everso.club. Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block anyone who
breaks this code.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
